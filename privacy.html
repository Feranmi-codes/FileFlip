PRIVACY

Your files are yours.

This Privacy Policy explains how FileFlip handles information when you use our website and browser-based file tools.

File processing

FileFlip's current PDF and image conversion tools are designed to process files directly in your browser where technically possible. Files processed by these tools are not intentionally uploaded to a FileFlip conversion server.

Because processing takes place in your browser, performance can depend on your device, browser and available memory.

Information you provide

If you contact FileFlip through our Contact page, you may provide information such as your name, email address and message. This information is used to respond to your request, provide support and address feedback or reported problems.

Contact form services

Our contact form may use a third-party form processing service to deliver messages to FileFlip. Information submitted through the contact form may therefore be processed by that service according to its own privacy practices.

Analytics and advertising

At the time of publication, FileFlip does not intentionally include advertising or analytics code on its website.

If analytics, advertising or other third-party services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant information practices.

Third-party links and services

FileFlip may use third-party services or link to external websites. Those services and websites have their own privacy policies and terms, which may differ from those of FileFlip.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when FileFlip adds new features, tools or third-party services. The updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

Have a privacy question?

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy or how FileFlip handles information, you can contact us.
