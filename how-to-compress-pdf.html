How to Compress a PDF Online

Large PDF files can be difficult to share or upload. Compressing a PDF can reduce its file size and make it easier to work with.

How to compress a PDF

Step 1: Select your PDF

Open the FileFlip PDF compressor and choose the PDF you want to reduce.

Step 2: Choose a compression level

FileFlip provides different compression levels so you can choose the balance between file size and image quality that works for your document.

Step 3: Compress the PDF

Click the Compress PDF button and wait while the document is processed.

Step 4: Download your compressed PDF

When the process is complete, download the new PDF and compare its size with the original if necessary.

Why compress a PDF?

A smaller PDF can be easier to upload to websites, attach to messages, store on your device, or share with other people.

Important note

FileFlip's current browser-based compressor recreates PDF pages as compressed images. This can significantly reduce file size for image-heavy or scanned documents, but text in the resulting PDF may no longer be selectable like the original document.
