How to Merge PDF Files Online

Combining several PDF files into one document can make files easier to organize, share and store.

How to merge multiple PDFs

Step 1: Choose your PDF files

Open the FileFlip Merge PDF tool and select two or more PDF files from your device.

Step 2: Check your files

The selected PDF files will appear in the tool. Check that you have selected the documents you want to combine.

Step 3: Merge the PDFs

Click Merge PDFs. FileFlip processes the selected documents and combines their pages into a single PDF.

Step 4: Download the merged document

When the process is finished, download your new PDF.

Why merge PDF files?

Merging PDFs can be useful when you have separate documents that belong together, such as multiple pages of a report, forms, notes or other documents.

Is there a file limit?

The current browser-based FileFlip merger does not require an account. Your device's available memory and the size of the PDF files can affect how many documents you can process at once.
